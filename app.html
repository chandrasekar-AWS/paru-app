Permissions

Paru only does what you allow. Change any of these at any time.

Assistant

Your mail, calendar, tasks and daily report. Paru drafts replies, and nothing is sent to a client until you press Send in Telegram.

The daily report covers yesterday 5 PM to today 5 PM and arrives at 5 PM. Paru cannot answer or end phone calls on this computer.

Keys

Your private keys. Saving restarts the agent (about 10 seconds).
